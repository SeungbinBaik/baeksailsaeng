Support

A nameless soldier dies on the battlefield and wakes with his memories. Hundred Deaths is a death-loop action RPG set in the Three Kingdoms era (iOS and Android).

  • Bug reports and contact: please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
  • New device: tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the unlock screen.
  • Save data is stored only on your device and is deleted with the app.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com), or by Google Play for the Android version.

Version 1.0 buyers: Origin Pack 1 is free

Origin Pack 1 "Child of Taiping" (Chapter 10, from version 1.2) is free for:

  • players who bought the Complete Edition on or before October 18, 2026;
  • players who bought both the Main Story and Expansion 1 in version 1.0.

After updating to 1.2, open it from the 「出身·太平道之子」 (「出身・太平道の子」) button on the main menu. If it does not appear, tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Eligibility is based on the original purchase date recorded by the App Store or Google Play; no code is needed.
