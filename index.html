Support

A nameless soldier dies on the battlefield and wakes with his memories. Hundred Deaths is a death-loop action RPG set in the Three Kingdoms era (iOS).

  • Bug reports and contact: please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
  • New device: tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the unlock screen.
  • Save data is stored only on your device and is deleted with the app.
